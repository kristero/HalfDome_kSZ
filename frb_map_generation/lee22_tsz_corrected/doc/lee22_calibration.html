Lee22 calibration in HalfDome

How the Lee et al. (2022, MNRAS 517, 420) IllustrisTNG electron density and pressure fits are corrected before painting HalfDome FRB dispersion measures, kSZ and tSZ maps. Status 2026-10-01 (version 4: claims re-verified, TNG mass windows read exactly).

In short

M1, the bookkeeping error. Lee22's extraction code divides particle sums by comoving shell volumes but normalises by the physical critical density. The printed fits are therefore low by a3 (density) and a2 (pressure), with a = 1/(1+z) (the pressure step is in the public code; the density's normalisation step is not public, so its a3 is inferred, and confirmed by CAMELS and FLAMINGO). Undoing it multiplies the density by (1+z)3 and the pressure by (1+z)2.

Fn and FP, what remains. After undoing M1 the printed density is still 5.1–5.9 times the TNG gas, the pressure is right (FP = 0.99–1.07 in the TNG model). FP = 1 follows from the public pressure code (y_prof.cpp), which computes P200 in the same h units as the profile, so h cancels. The density counts are per (ckpc/h)3 and were normalised outside the public code: reading them as per physical kpc3 gives the a3 and a 1/h3, and an XH on the wrong side of the printed n200 gives 1/XH2. Fn = 1/(h3XH2) = 5.57 is not fitted; it is the candidate the measured 5.1–5.9 selects.

What HalfDome uses. Pressure: Lee22 × (1+z)2. Density: Lee22 × [0.6774 (1+z)]3 XH2. The empirical corrections used on the way (C(z) = 0.284 (1+z)2.48 from a TNG DM catalogue whose "1013–1014" array also holds the 1014–1015 decade, and Fn = 5.4 from the CAMELS gas) are not final products.

Consequences. FRB halo DM: each "A to B" array of the TNG catalogue spans [10A, 10B+1) M☉, so its 1013–1014 decade is DM(10to13) − DM(10to12) ray by ray. Against it the derived density reproduces TNG's mean halo DM per hit to 2.5 % (0.98–1.02) at zs = 0.1–3 (HalfDome rays); Battaglia16 gives 0.86–1.02 and the previous ×0.6016 density 1.03–2.85 (it equals the derived one only at halo redshift z ≈ 0.5). tSZ: Lee22 × (1+z)2 is 2.3–3× FLAMINGO's y power. At ℓ ≲ 2000 most of the excess comes from the ~1,200 halos above 1014.8 h-1M☉ (Lee22's fitted bins end near 1014); with Battaglia12 there it is 1.15–1.5× at ℓ ≤ 3000 (1.8× at ℓ = 5000), and at ℓ ≳ 3000 much of the excess comes from halos below 1014.8. Against Takahashi+25 the corrected pairs remove the previous failure (χ² 20–24 / 10–23 for 8 / 11 bins vs 58.8 / 119.4 before). In Planck no HalfDome pair, nor w = 0, fits in absolute terms; in ACT the corrected pairs are acceptable once the Hartlap factor for the 31-FRB jackknife is applied, although the full Lee22 × (1+z)2 pair is disfavoured relative to Battaglia (Δχ² = 14, 8.4 with the Hartlap factor).

1. The Lee22 fits as printed

Lee22 fit the mean electron density and electron pressure of TNG300 halos (Rockstar gravity-only parent halos; 0.2-dex bins whose concentration quartiles hold more than 29 halos, from 1013.0 to about 1014.0 h-1M☉ at z ≤ 0.2 (the paper: “up to M = 1014 h-1M☉”; per-bin z = 0 fits reach 1014.4), about 1013.6 at z = 1 and 1013.2 at z = 2; 0.04–1.34 R200c in 27 bins; the full snapshots at z = 0–2, 10 of TNG's 20) with one generalised NFW shape. We use the fits without concentration (Tables 7 and 8):

ne(x) / n200 = n0 (x/xc)γ [1 + (x/xc)α]−β′,   Pe(x) / P200 = P0 (x/xc)γ [1 + (x/xc)α]−β x = r/R200c, α = 1, γ = −0.3; each parameter A = A0 (M/Mcut)αm (1+z)αz, the mass slope allowed to break at Mcut = 1013.61 h-1M☉ (density; broken in x′c and β′) and 1013.60 h-1M☉ (pressure; broken in P0); e.g. n0 = 6.8 (M/Mcut)0.68(1+z)−2.11, P0 = 2.8 (M/Mcut)1.08(1+z)−1.89 below Mcut, mass slope 0.80 above
n200 = 200 ρcr(z) fb / (XH mp),   P200 = 200 G M200 ρcr(z) fb / (2 R200) ρcr(z) = 3H02[Ωm(1+z)3 + ΩΛ]/(8πG) and R200c defined by it, i.e. physical; fb = Ωb/Ωm (paper eq. 5 and 9)

Our transcription matches Tables 7 and 8 exactly and reproduces the paper's own fit curves (vector paths from the arXiv source figures) to 0.5–3.6 % at the bin-centre mass (median 2–3 %); with the mass moved by ≤ 0.015 dex and the amplitude by 0–4 % (rounding of the printed parameters) the drawn curves are reproduced to < 0.05 %. Fig. 1 replicates the paper's Fig. 2 and Fig. 5 with the paper's own TNG profile, digitized from the vector figures together with its error bars. The residual rows use the paper's definitions, which the digitization reproduces exactly: Δ = (Asim − Amodel)/Asim, with σ the error of the mean (red bars; the blue bars are the halo scatter; ≈ 177 and ≈ 65 halos as estimated from (scatter/σ)2 (the second is more than a quartile of TNG300's ≈ 165 halos in that bin would hold), the lowest-concentration quartile of each bin that the paper's figures show; the profile is drawn at 103 radii, the bars at every second one).

Exponent convention. The printed fits write the outer factor as [1 + (x/xc)α]−β′. XGPaint's generalized_nfw uses the exponent −(β+γ)/α, so we pass βXG = αβ′ − γ, the same mapping XGPaint applies to Battaglia12. Against the paper's own drawn fit curves, this reading reproduces the shape to 0.3–3 % (max/min of model/paper over 0.04–1.34 R200c, with a 0.5–3.6 % offset); reading β′ as an asymptotic slope, −(β′ ∓ γ)/α, tilts the curves by 6–19 % (doc/check_beta_convention.py). Lee22's printed β′ is therefore not a mistake: it is the exponent their own fit curves use. An old XGPaint sign bug in the Battaglia12 exponent ((1+x)+β, from 9f37b1e, merged in b924ab1 on 2026-03-02, fixed in e67a8a1 on 2026-04-30) is in none of the maps used here: the local copy of the B12 lookup table equals the correct-sign profile (the idark copy that painted the maps was not read, but the buggy sign would change y by 1029–1040), and the maps' Battaglia12 / FLAMINGO power is 0.70–0.80.

The Battaglia curves drawn in Lee22 are not what HalfDome paints. Read from the vector figures, their Battaglia12 curve is the thermal pressure with the h-1M☉ mass used as M☉ (matches to 0.2 %), so it is 1/0.5176 = 1.93× Battaglia12's own electron pressure, the quantity the simulation curve shows (Battaglia+12: Pth = 1.932 Pe). Their Battaglia16 curve is Battaglia's appendix formula exactly as printed – ρgas = ρcritρfit without fb and exponent −(β−γ)/α – in units of 200 fbρcr (i.e. ρfit/(200 fb), no electron conversion), again with h-1M☉ as M☉ (matches to 0.1 %). Both departures – the missing fb and the −(β−γ)/α exponent – are typos in Battaglia (2016) itself, which Lee22 copied: its own Fig. A1 is reproduced by its Table A1 only with ρgas = fbρcritρfit and −(β+γ)/α, the two corrections XGPaint applies. The drawn curve is therefore 1/fb = 6.4× too high, has a tilted outer slope, and lies 6–9× above the Battaglia16 ne HalfDome paints. The Battaglia profiles as painted by XGPaint lie within 4–5 % of TNG at 0.3 < x < 1 in the lower bin and 24–28 % below it in the upper bin.

2. M1: why (1+z)3 and (1+z)2

The pressure was extracted with the public group_particles example y_prof.cpp, which the first author generalised to all snapshots. The density was extracted with an electron-density version of ne_prof.cpp that is not public; the public ne_prof.cpp covers only z = 0 and M > 1014 h-1M☉. The paper used Rockstar gravity-only halos and 27 bins in 0.04–1.34 R200c. TNG snapshots store lengths in comoving kpc/h, and both codes build the shells from comoving halo radii (Group_R_Crit200 in the examples, Rockstar R200c in the paper, both ckpc/h), while y_prof.cpp normalises by the physical critical density at the snapshot time:

group_particles/examples/y_prof.cpp (Lee22 extraction)
// shells between logRmin..logRmax, from Group_R_Crit200 (ckpc/h)
return 4.0*M_PI/3.0*( std::exp(3.0*logR2) - std::exp(3.0*logR1) );
// per particle: electron pressure x particle volume
auto Y = 4.0*x*XH/(1.0+3.0*XH+4.0*XH*x) * (gamma-1.0) * m * e;
pressure[ii] /= shell_vol(ii);       // divided by a COMOVING volume

grp_P_t rho_crit () const {
    grp_P_t a3 = Time*Time*Time;
    return rho_crit_0*(Omega0/a3 + OmegaLambda); // PHYSICAL
}
// P200 with the comoving r = Group_R_Crit200
return 100.0*GNewton*m*rho_crit()*OmegaBaryon/Omega0/r;

auto ne = x * XH * m / mprot;       // electrons in the cell
e_density[idx] += ne;
...
e_density[ii] /= shell_vol(ii);      // COMOVING (ckpc/h)^3
// n200 = 200 rho_crit(z) f_b/(X_H m_p) is applied later, outside this code (not public);
// the measured (1+z)^3 and 1/h^3 imply a PHYSICAL, h-free rho_crit there

Counting powers of a = 1/(1+z), with Vcom = Vphys/a3 and Rcom = Rphys/a:

nprinted / n200 = (N / Vcom) / n200 = a3 · ntrue/n200 density: only the volume carries a
Pprinted / P200,code = (a3 Ptrue) / (a P200) = a2 · Ptrue/P200 pressure: a3 from the volume, a−1 from the comoving R200 in the denominator of P200

So the true profiles are the printed ones times (1+z)3 and (1+z)2. For the pressure this is an identity of y_prof.cpp, valid at every redshift and equal to 1 at z = 0; for the density it is inferred, because the n200 step is not public, and confirmed by measurement. One consistency check and two independent tests:

  • Emulation. Applying the same bookkeeping to CAMELS-TNG gas cells gives emulated/true = a3 and a2 to about 1 % at z = 0–2 (e.g. 0.1169 vs a3 = 0.1164 and 0.2390 vs a2 = 0.2384 at z = 1.05; 0.4704 vs 0.4659 for the pressure at z = 0.47).
  • TNG model (CAMELS, 27 boxes). TNG / printed = 1.02 (1+z)1.84 ± 0.06 for the pressure (point ratios 1.007 and 4.012 at z = 0 and 1, i.e. (1+z)2.0; 12 halos at z = 1, none at z = 2) and (1+z)3.0–3.1 for the density (1013.5–1014 M☉).
  • FLAMINGO (a different code and feedback model): the redshift slope of the printed fit is lower by 1.97–2.07 (pressure) and 2.9–3.1 (density) above 1013.6 M☉.

3. What is left after M1: Fn from the code units, FP = 1

After undoing M1, any constant offset between the fit and the TNG gas is a measured ratio. Both are defined the same way:

Fn = nprinted(1+z)3 / nsim,   FP = Pprinted(1+z)2 / Psim mean over 0.04–1.34 R200c; measured values in the table below

The measurements are FP ≈ 1 and Fn ≈ 5.5. Neither is used as a fitted constant. FP = 1 follows from the units of y_prof.cpp; Fn follows from the (ckpc/h)3 counts plus two inferred properties of the non-public n200 step (an h-free physical n200, and XH in the numerator), which the measured Fn selects among the candidates below. The difference comes from where each profile is normalised.

group_particles/examples/ne_prof.cpp — density: counted in code units, normalised elsewhere

// outside this code (not public)

density: electrons per (ckpc/h)3 read as per physical kpc3 → nstored = ntrue (a/h)3: if so, the printed fit is low by a3 and high by 1/h3 Vphys = (a/h)3 Vcode: the a3 is M1, the 1/h3 = 3.22 is part of the same unit mistake
pressure: numerically Pe,code = Pe a3/h2 and P200,code = P200 a/h2 (both in units ∝ h2) → h cancels; only a3/a = a2 survives this is why the pressure needs no constant (FP = 1) while the density does: h cancels, and P200 carries no XH convention that could be misprinted (the per-cell electron fraction 4xXH/(1+3XH+4XHx) is exact)

The rest of Fn is attributed to the hydrogen convention. The paper counts electrons as Vine,i = ximiXH/mp (x = ElectronAbundance = ne/nH), which equals the hydrogen count times x; the measured Fn selects normalisation by the hydrogen density nH,200 = 200 ρcrfbXH/mp. The paper prints n200 = 200 ρcrfb/(XHmp), which is neither the hydrogen nor the electron density. If Lee22 normalised by nH,200 (XH in the numerator, the opposite of the printed n200), reading the printed form literally overestimates ne by 1/XH2 = 1.73.

FP = 0.99–1.07 in the TNG model (CAMELS; Lee22 fitted TNG300), as the h-cancellation predicts; FLAMINGO's 20 % lower group pressure is a real difference between the simulations. Below 1013.5 M☉ both ratios drift: the bin reaches below the fit's lower mass limit (1013 h-1M☉ = 1.5 × 1013 M☉), and its Fn still uses the all-mass electron fraction (biased high by up to ~15 %). At z = 0, where M1 vanishes, Fig. 1 shows the factor directly: Lee22's own TNG density curve matches their fit, and both are 5.9× the CAMELS-TNG gas, between 5.57 and Ωm/Ωb = 6.36, so Fig. 1 alone does not choose between them (correcting the 1–5 % low coarse CAMELS bins moves it towards 5.57); the pressure curves agree. The constant is therefore in Lee22's conversion of the counts, not in the fitting. Neither candidate is a physical gas-fraction effect: both are bookkeeping. 1/(h3XH2) is preferred because each factor has a mechanism in the code and paper (the (ckpc/h)3 counts give 1/h3 for an h-free n200; an XH misplaced in eq. 9 gives 1/XH2) and because it matches the 1013.5–1014 M☉ values; Ωm/Ωb would need fb twice in n200, has no code path and is 14–25 % above those values. Both are constant in z, so the redshift trend cannot separate them.

The earlier empirical corrections

Two empirical corrections were used on the way to this result and are no longer final products. C(z) = 0.284 (1+z)2.48 was fitted to the TNG300 FRB halo-DM catalogue of Konietzka et al. ("1013–1014 M☉"), and Fn = 5.4 to the CAMELS gas. Fn = 5.4 agrees with the derived 5.57 to 3 %. C(z) does not, because the catalogue window contains clusters: on every ray DM(10to14) = DM(10to12) + DM(13to14) to 10−12 pc cm−3, which requires the upper edge of "10to12" to be the lower edge of "13to14"; with the hit fractions (DM(10to14) − DM(10to13) is hit at the 1014–1015 rate, not the 1013–1014 one) this fixes each "A to B" array as [10A, 10B+1) M☉, so the "1013–1014" array holds halos of 1013–1015 M☉. The nominal 1014–1015 difference DM(10to15) − DM(10to14) is non-zero on only 0.065 / 0.23 / 0.26 % of rays at zs = 0.1 / 0.5 / 1 (it is the 1015–1016 decade). The 1014–1015 decade, DM(10to14) − DM(10to13), is hit on 2.0 / 8.3 / 11.7 % of rays, as in HalfDome (2.0 / 8.7 / 11.9 %; 2.1 / 8.8 / 12.1 % for 1014–1016). The nominal windows overlap by one decade. No boundary or electron convention (R200m, FoF cells, 57 ρcr cells, star-forming gas) reproduces both the extra electrons and the equal hit fractions; counting the clusters does, and then the catalogue needs Fn = 5.4 (1+z)−0.1, as the TNG gas does. The reading follows exactly from the arrays (the identity holds on every ray); R. Konietzka should confirm the labelling convention (decades A..B inclusive).

const LEE2022_TNG_CALIBRATION_C0 = 0.2841790493880425      # C(z) fitted to Konietzka TNG300 halo DM
const LEE2022_TNG_CALIBRATION_K  = 2.483190184608004
const LEE2022_BOOKKEEPING_FN = parse(Float64, get(ENV, "LEE2022_BOOKKEEPING_FN", "5.4"))
const LEE2022_TNG_LITTLE_H = 0.6774                         # h hard-coded in Lee22's mprot

function lee2022_redshift_scaling_factor(model, z)
    model.redshift_scaling == :physical && return 1.0            # eq. 9 as printed
    model.redshift_scaling == :bookkeeping_physical &&                # final: derived, no fit
        return (LEE2022_TNG_LITTLE_H * (1 + z))^3 * model.hydrogen_mass_fraction^2
    model.redshift_scaling == :bookkeeping_a3 && return (1 + z)^3 / LEE2022_BOOKKEEPING_FN   # empirical check
    model.redshift_scaling == :tng_calibrated &&
        return LEE2022_TNG_CALIBRATION_C0 * (1 + min(z, 2.0))^LEE2022_TNG_CALIBRATION_K
    ...
end

XGPaint src/profiles_y.jl — the shared shape function (why βXG = αβ′ − γ)
function generalized_nfw(x, xc, α, β, γ)
    x̄ = x / xc
    return x̄^γ * (1 + x̄^α)^(-(β + γ) / α)   # with β = αβ′ − γ: exponent −β′, as printed
end

5. Results

FRB halo DM: HalfDome rays, analytic halo model and TNG

120,000 rays, gas inside each halo's R200c sphere. The analytic curves are the Torkamani et al. (2026) compound-Poisson halo model with clustering, evaluated with exactly the same densities. With the HalfDome halo abundance it reproduces the rays' mean DM per hit to ≤ 1 % at zs = 0.5 and 1 and to 2–3 % at zs = 0.1 for the Lee22 densities (Battaglia16 at zs = 0.1: 8 % low), so for these densities rays and analytic model are interchangeable; in the TNG cosmology with a Tinker08 mass function down to 109 M☉ it is the analytic model for TNG's cosmology (TNG300's own halo abundance and the catalogue's window labels are treated in the analytic-reproduction subsection).

Analytic reproduction of TNG (as in Torkamani+26 and Medlock+24)

What the papers compared. Torkamani et al. (2026) test only the total TNG300 DM distribution (IGM + halos, gas to 5 R200c, halos 108–1016 h-1M☉, mean taken from the Macquart relation): MCMC fits at z = 0.7, 1.5, 3, 4, 5 (reduced χ² 1.51 / 0.30 / 0.22 / 0.40 / 1.17) and fiducial-parameter PDFs at z = 0.2–0.6 (good down to z ≈ 0.3). Their only halo-level test is of the gas density profiles, not of halo DM. Medlock et al. (2024) use no halo model; they estimate F = σ/⟨DM⟩·√z = 0.182 at z = 0.5 from 10,000 CAMELS-TNG sightlines (25 h-1 Mpc boxes; biased low by cosmic variance, they note) and show halocentric DM profiles in 1011–1013 M☉ bins. Neither compares a halo-only DM PDF or the catalogue's mass windows with an analytic halo model.

Their test reproduces. Our engine with Torkamani's BFC parameters matches the TNG total-DM distribution to KS distance 0.03–0.05 (zs = 0.3–1), 10–14 % narrower; the Macquart form does not, for any F (KS ≈ 0.10 at F = 0.182, ≥ 0.08 at its best F); TNG300's σ/⟨DM⟩·√z is 0.245 at z = 0.5. The BFC model is shifted to the TNG mean, as Torkamani add the mean separately. This test says little about halo interiors: BFC puts 83 % of its halo DM outside R200c.

Halo-only. With the "1013–1014" array read as [1013, 1015) M☉ (computed with 9 × 1014; < 1 % difference) and TNG300's hydro halo abundance (Pillepich+18b counts, ≈ 0.8 of Tinker08 there, confirmed independently by the ray hit fractions, 0.81), the derived Lee22 density gives 0.96 / 0.97 / 0.97 of the TNG mean over all rays (1.02 / 1.01 / 0.99 per hit ray) at zs = 0.1 / 0.5 / 1, KS 0.02–0.025, flat in zs; with a plain Tinker08 abundance the mean is 1.15–1.17 but the per-hit mean (0.98–1.04) and KS (0.02–0.03) are unchanged; Battaglia16 and BFC give ≈ 0.8 and the previous ×0.6016 falls from 2.8 to 1.4. Read exactly (no reinterpretation, no abundance correction, per hit), the analytic model with the derived density in the TNG cosmology gives 0.95 / 1.01 / 1.03 of the mean DM per hit of TNG's 1013–1014 decade (table above). For the window-free "all halos" array every smooth profile falls short with TNG300's hit-calibrated abundance (derived Lee22 0.67 / 0.61 / 0.53, Battaglia16 0.91 / 0.83 / 0.73; with plain Tinker08 the derived Lee22 is still 0.81 / 0.74 / 0.64, while Battaglia16 reaches 0.94 at zs = 1): 58 % of TNG's DM at zs = 1 comes from halos below 1013 M☉, where TNG implies 0.33 / 0.53 / 0.47 fb of electrons inside R200c for 1010–11 / 1011–12 / 1012–13 M☉ (0.23–0.41 if its abundance were Tinker08), against 0.01 / 0.05 / 0.21 for the extrapolated Lee22 fit.

Not the finite box. TNG300 (205 Mpc/h) holds 278 / 152 / 49 halos of 1014–1015 M☉ at z = 0 / 0.5 / 1. The ray hit fraction depends on their number density, not on the box volume, so a lightcone built from these snapshots should see such a halo on 2.0 / 8.3 / 11.6 % of rays (99.9 % range over box realisations 1.1–2.9 / 4.7–12.2 / 6.4–17 %). The catalogue's DM(10to15) − DM(10to14) is non-zero on only 0.065 / 0.23 / 0.26 %, 31–44× fewer, because it is the 1015–1016 decade (TNG300's ~3 halos above 1015 M☉ give ~0.07 / 0.21 / 0.23 %). The 1014–1015 decade, DM(10to14) − DM(10to13), is hit on 2.01 / 8.31 / 11.73 % of rays, exactly the expected rate: no cluster DM is missing.

tSZ: y power and tSZ × FRB DM

Five NSIDE 4096 y maps were painted in one catalogue pass on idark: Battaglia12, Lee22 as printed, Lee22 × (1+z)2, the hybrid (Battaglia12 above 1014.8 h-1M☉) and the calibrated-range map (Lee22 × (1+z)2 only for 1013–1014.8 h-1M☉ and z ≤ 2, other halos omitted; Lee22's fitted bins end near 1014), all with exact per-halo profiles for the 79,302 halos below z = 0.1. Four of the maps (all but the hybrid, for which no catalogue sum was computed) match the exact per-halo sum over the catalogue to within 5 % (0.95–1.01) at ℓ = 100–3000. The low-z fix changes the Battaglia12 power by −19 / −8 / −4 / −1 / −0.1 % at ℓ = 10 / 20 / 30 / 50 / 100 and nothing above ℓ = 300, exactly as predicted (to 10−4).
