### J.2 Definitions and equations

**Measured profiles (FLAMINGO, z = 0).** With $x = r/R_{200c}$:
$$E(x)=\frac{n_e(x)\,\mu_{\rm XGP}}{f_b^{\rm F}\,\rho_c^{\rm F}},\qquad P(x)=\frac{P_e(x)}{P_{200}},\qquad P_{200}=\frac{G\,M_{200c}\,200\,\rho_c^{\rm F}\,f_b^{\rm F}}{2R_{200c}}$$
- $n_e$: free-electron number density [m^-3]; $P_e$: electron pressure [Pa];
- $\mu_{\rm XGP}=(m_e+2m_p/1.76)/0.9\approx1.2632\,m_p$: XGPaint's gas mass per electron [kg];
- $f_b^{\rm F}=\Omega_b/\Omega_m$ = 0.15882: FLAMINGO's cosmic baryon fraction [results/parity.json: chord.f_b_study]; $\rho_c^{\rm F}$: FLAMINGO's critical density at z = 0 [kg m^-3];
- $G$: Newton's constant; $M_{200c}$ [kg] and $R_{200c}$ [m]: mass and radius enclosing 200 $\rho_c^{\rm F}$;
- $E$ and $P$ are dimensionless. A shell value $F_{h,s}$ ($F$ = $E$ or $P$) is the volume average of halo $h$ in fine shell $s=[x_s,x_{s+1}]$.

**Painting in the engine (HalfDome lightcone, observer at z = 0).** A lightcone halo of mass $M$ at redshift $z$ in calibrated bin $c$ is painted with the bin's z = 0 stack and the published redshift response:
$$n_e(r)=n_{\rm u}(z)\,E_c(x)\,e_E(M,z,x),\qquad n_{\rm u}(z)=\frac{f_b^{\rm HD}\rho_c^{\rm HD}(z)}{\mu_{\rm XGP}},\qquad e_E(M,z,x)=\frac{F^{\rm B16}(M,z,x)}{F^{\rm B16}(M,0,x)}$$
$$F^{\rm B16}(M,z,x)=A\,(x/0.5)^{-0.2}\big[1+(x/0.5)^{\alpha}\big]^{-(\beta-0.2)/\alpha},\quad A=4000\,m^{0.29}(1+z)^{-0.66},\ \alpha=0.88\,m^{-0.03}(1+z)^{0.19},\ \beta=3.83\,m^{0.04}(1+z)^{-0.025}$$
- $f_b^{\rm HD}=0.049/0.31$ = 0.15806: HalfDome's baryon fraction used by the engine (`lightcone_analytic.FB`), not FLAMINGO's; at fixed $M$ the painted electron count is therefore 0.9952 times the FLAMINGO-measured one (the stacks fix $E$, i.e. the electron density relative to $f_b\rho_c$);
- $\rho_c^{\rm HD}(z)$: HalfDome's critical density at the halo redshift (saved CLASS tables, h = 0.68) [kg m^-3]; $R_{200c}$ of the lightcone halo follows from $M$ and $\rho_c^{\rm HD}(z)$;
- $n_{\rm u}(z)$: the electron-density unit [m^-3];
- $e_E$: the declared redshift response (dimensionless; $e_E=1$ at z = 0). It rescales the z = 0 stack by the published B16 evolution at the halo's own mass, so every DM number depends on it;
- $F^{\rm B16}$: the published Battaglia (2016) gas-density profile in units of $f_b\rho_c$; $m=M/(10^{14}\,M_\odot)$; coefficients `pilot_physics.B16` (`radii234_contamination_L1_m9_0077_20261008/pilot_physics.py:21`), used by `lightcone_analytic.published_profile`;
- pressure is painted the same way: $P_e=P_{200}^{\rm HD}(M,z)\,P_c(x)\,e_P(M,z,x)$ with $e_P=F^{\rm B12}(M,z,x)/F^{\rm B12}(M,0,x)$, $F^{\rm B12}=0.5176\,P_0\,(x/x_c)^{-0.3}(1+x/x_c)^{-\beta}$, $P_0=18.1\,m^{0.154}(1+z)^{-0.758}$, $x_c=0.497\,m^{-0.00865}(1+z)^{0.731}$, $\beta=4.35\,m^{0.0393}(1+z)^{0.415}$ (0.5176 converts thermal to electron pressure), and $P_{200}^{\rm HD}$ the expression above with $f_b^{\rm HD}$ and $\rho_c^{\rm HD}(z)$.
- Uncalibrated (fallback) haloes are painted with $F^{\rm B16}$ / $F^{\rm B12}$ themselves.

**A2-ii partition.** A gas particle at position $\mathbf r_i$ belongs to the painted halo $h^*=\arg\min_h|\mathbf r_i-\mathbf r_h|/R_{200c,h}$ over the halo and its painted neighbours ($M_{200c}\ge6.6\times10^{12}\,M_\odot$). **Host-bound:** gas bound (HBT) to the central or to its own satellites, at any radius to 4 R200c; it is not a subset of A2-ii.

**Cosmic mean in E units.** $\bar E=\kappa_E\,\Omega_m$ = 0.3348 [results/stacks.npz: E_cosmic]:
- $\kappa_E$ = 1.0940: electrons per unit gas mass in E units, i.e. $\mu_{\rm XGP}$ divided by the true gas mass per free electron, measured at 2-4 R200c (`G_physical_outer/results/prepare.json`: normalisation.KAPPA_E_measured_2_4R);
- $\Omega_m=\Omega_b/f_b^{\rm F}$ = 0.306: FLAMINGO's matter density parameter.

**Stack.**
$$\bar F_{b,s}=\frac{\sum_{h\in b}w_hF_{h,s}}{\sum_{h\in b}w_h}$$
- $b$: 0.2-dex M200c bin; $s$: fine shell; $w_h$: inclusion weight of halo $h$ (inverse inclusion probability of its stratum); $F_{h,s}$: the halo's shell average of $P$ or $E$ (dimensionless).

**Stack-level rule (12.7-13.5 only).** Inside each segment $[0,x_h]$, $[x_h,1]$, $[1,2]$, $[2,4]$, consecutive fine shells are merged greedily from the inside out into a group $G$ until
$$N_{b,G}=\sum_{h\in b}\sum_{s\in G}n_{h,s}\ \ge\ 400 .$$
- $n_{h,s}$: gas particles of halo $h$ in shell $s$ owned by the selection, AGN-heated gas excluded (A2-ii counts for $P$ and $E$, host-bound counts for D3); $N_{b,G}$: the stack's particle count in group $G$;
- $1/\sqrt{400}$ = 5% is the Poisson error of the stacked particle sum; a remainder below 400 at the end of a segment joins the previous group;
- per halo, the merged value $F_{h,G}=\sum_{s\in G}V_sF_{h,s}/\sum_{s\in G}V_s$, with $V_s=\tfrac{4\pi}{3}(x_{s+1}^3-x_s^3)$ the dimensionless shell volume, is assigned to every fine shell of $G$ (treatment T1, baseline). Bins >= 13.5 keep the raw fine shells, as in Test H.

**Hydrodynamic-resolution radius.** $x_h$ = the first fine edge with $x_s\ge\mathrm{median}_h(2h_{h,s}/R_{200c})$, where $h_{h,s}$ is the upper-median SPH smoothing length of the halo's gas in shell $s$. Inside $x_h$ the radial shape is unresolved but the integral is measured.

**Treatments of the unresolved cores** (all three keep each halo's measured integral inside $x_h$):
- T2: inside $x_h$, $F_{h,s}=S_s\,X_h(<x_h)/\sum_{s<k_h}V_sS_s$, with $S_s$ the published shell average (B16 for $E$, B12 for $P$) inclusion-weighted over the bin, $k_h$ the index of the shell starting at $x_h$, and $X_h(<x_h)=\sum_{s<k_h}V_sF_{h,s}$ the halo's measured integral inside $x_h$;
- T3: raw fine shells (no merging);
- S82: bin 12.7-12.9 built only from its 113 haloes with M200c >= 6.6e12 Msun; their mean log M is 12.860, against 12.792 for the full bin and 12.871 for the bin's lightcone haloes at all z and 12.891 at z <= 1 [post/lightcone_mass_z.json]. S82 is therefore still below the painted z <= 1 masses (smallest 12.869).

**Integrals.**
$$X(<R)=\sum_{x_{s+1}\le R}V_s\bar F_s,\qquad \frac{N_e(<R)\,\mu_{\rm XGP}}{f_b^{\rm F}M_{200c}}=\frac{X_E(<R)}{800\pi/3}=f_e(<R)$$
- $R$: radius in units of R200c; $X_E$ ($X_P$): the integral of $E$ ($P$) in units of $f_b^{\rm F}\rho_c^{\rm F}R_{200c}^3/\mu_{\rm XGP}$ ($P_{200}R_{200c}^3$); $N_e(<R)$: electrons inside $R$; the identity uses $M_{200c}=200\rho_c^{\rm F}\tfrac{4\pi}{3}R_{200c}^3$; $f_e$: electrons in units of the cosmic-baryon-fraction expectation. $Y(<R)=X_P(<R)$ in units of $P_{200}R_{200c}^3$.

**Halo-component definitions.** D0: A2-ii to 4 R200c. D1: $F^{\rm D1}_s=F^{\rm A2ii}_s\,\mathbb 1[x_{s+1}\le1]$, a 3D cut. D2: the same cut at $x=2$. D3: host-bound to 4 R200c. The fallback haloes above 10^15.388 are cut at the same radius as the definition.

**Projection of a 3D-cut profile.**
$$\Sigma(b)=2\int_0^{\sqrt{X^2-b^2}}E\big(\sqrt{b^2+l^2}\big)\,e_E\,dl,\qquad \mathrm{DM}(b)=\frac{R_{200c}\,n_{\rm u}(z)}{1+z}\,\Sigma(b)=\frac{2R_{200c}}{1+z}\int_0^{\sqrt{X^2-b^2}}n_e\big(\sqrt{b^2+l^2}\big)\,dl$$
- $b$: impact parameter in units of R200c; $l$: line-of-sight coordinate in units of R200c; $X$: sphere radius (1, 2 or 4); $\Sigma$: dimensionless column; DM in pc cm^-3 (observer frame, factor $1/(1+z)$); $R_{200c}n_{\rm u}(z)/(1+z)$ is the column scale (pc cm^-3 per unit $\Sigma$).
- This is HalfDome's SphericalChordDMProfile formula (`frb_map_generation/lee2022_frb_dm_profile.jl:681-751`). Gate G8, B16 profile at log M = 13.6, z = 0.3, X = 1 [results/parity.json: chord]: the engine column (exact projection over the basis segments inside X) equals the chord integral of the same node profile to 2.5e-09 and the smooth B16 to 8.5e-05; column scale x Sigma equals the formula in SI units to 8.9e-16; the column scale there is 0.4430 pc cm^-3, computed with $f_b^{\rm HD}$.

**Fourier transforms of a halo** (multipole $\ell$; flat-sky, Limber):
$$\tau_\ell(M,z)=\sigma_T\,n_{\rm u}(z)\,\frac{R_{200c}^3}{d_A^2}\,4\pi\int_0^{X}x^2E(x)\,e_E(M,z,x)\,\frac{\sin(qx)}{qx}\,dx,\qquad q=\frac{(\ell+1/2)R_{200c}}{d_A}$$
- $\tau_\ell$: transform of the halo's optical-depth map (dimensionless); $\sigma_T$: Thomson cross-section [m^2]; $d_A$: angular-diameter distance [m]; $X$: outer radius of the definition; $q$: dimensionless wavenumber.
- DM: $\mathrm{DM}_\ell=s_{\rm DM}(z)\,\tau_\ell$ with $s_{\rm DM}(z)=1/[\sigma_T\,u_{\rm DM}\,(1+z)]$ for $z\le1$ and 0 beyond (sources at $z_s=1$); $u_{\rm DM}=3.0857\times10^{22}$ m^-2 is 1 pc cm^-3.
- tSZ: $y_\ell$ is the same transform of $\sigma_T P_e/(m_ec^2)$ ($m_e$ electron mass, $c$ speed of light).
- In the fine basis every transform is linear in the shell values: $\tau_\ell=\sum_s E_{c,s}\,g_{s,\ell}(M,z)$, with $g_{s,\ell}$ the transform of a unit step profile in shell $s$ (including $e_E$).

**Moments.**
$$\langle\mathrm{DM}\rangle=\sum_c\sum_sE_{c,s}\,m_{c,s}+\mathrm{fb},\qquad \mathrm{Var(DM)}=\sum_c\sum_{s,t}Q_c[s,t]\,\langle E_sE_t\rangle_c+\mathrm{fb}$$
$$C_\ell^{1h}=\sum_c\sum_{s,t}K_c[s,t,\ell]\,\langle F_sF_t\rangle_c+\mathrm{fb},\qquad C_\ell^{2h}=\sum_z\frac{P_{\rm lin}(k_\ell,z)}{V_z}\,a_z\Big[W_{\rm fb}(z,\ell)+\sum_{c,s}F_{c,s}W_{c,s}(z,\ell)\Big]^2$$
- $c$: calibrated bin; cells: lightcone (z, M) histogram cells with $N$ haloes over the full sky; fb: the same sums over the fallback haloes;
- $m_{c,s}=\sum_{{\rm cells}\in c,\,z\le1}\frac{N}{4\pi}s_{\rm DM}(z)\,g_{s,0}$: mean DM per unit $E$ in shell $s$ [pc cm^-3];
- $Q_c[s,t]=\sum_{{\rm cells}\in c}\sum_{b}\lambda_b\,\mathrm{DM}_s(b)\,\mathrm{DM}_t(b)$: compound-Poisson (Campbell) variance kernel [(pc cm^-3)^2], $\mathrm{DM}_s(b)$ the DM at impact node $b$ of a unit profile in shell $s$, $\lambda_b=\frac{N}{4\pi}(R_{200c}/d_A)^2\,\Delta A_b$ the expected number of haloes per sightline with impact parameter in node $b$ ($\Delta A_b$ its area in units of $R_{200c}^2$);
- $\langle F_sF_t\rangle_c$: inclusion-weighted per-halo second moments (scatter-inclusive);
- $K_c[s,t,\ell]=\sum_{{\rm cells}\in c}\frac{N}{4\pi}\,g_{s,\ell}g_{t,\ell}$ for tSZ ($y$ transforms), the DM field ($s_{\rm DM}^2$ times the $\tau$ transforms) and y x DM; for the kSZ 1-halo term $N$ is replaced by $\sum_{h\in{\rm cell}}(v_{{\rm LOS},h}/c)^2$ with the catalogue line-of-sight velocities $v_{\rm LOS}$;
- $k_\ell=(\ell+1/2)/\chi(z)$: Limber wavenumber, $\chi$ the comoving distance; $P_{\rm lin}$: CLASS linear matter power spectrum [Mpc^3]; $V_z=(\chi_{\rm hi}^3-\chi_{\rm lo}^3)/3$: comoving volume of lightcone redshift shell $z$ per steradian [Mpc^3 sr^-1], $\chi_{\rm lo}$ and $\chi_{\rm hi}$ the comoving distances of its edges (`kernels/engine.py:494`), matching the per-steradian halo counts $N/4\pi$ in $W$;
- $W_{c,s}(z,\ell)=\sum_{M\in c}\frac{N}{4\pi}\,b_h(M,z)\,g_{s,\ell}(M,z)$: 2-halo window per unit shell value (Tinker bias $b_h$); $W_{\rm fb}$: the same sum over the fallback haloes with their published profiles;
- $a_z$ = 1 (tSZ), $\sigma^2_{v,{\rm lin}}(z)/c^2$ (kSZ, approximate 2-halo term; $\sigma_{v,{\rm lin}}$ the linear line-of-sight velocity dispersion), $s_{\rm DM}(z)^2$ (DM field), $s_{\rm DM}$ on one side only for y x DM;
- units: kSZ $C_\ell$ in $(\Delta T/T)^2$, $D_\ell=\ell(\ell+1)C_\ell T_{\rm CMB}^2/2\pi$ in muK^2 with $T_{\rm CMB}$ = 2.7255 K; DM-field $C_\ell$ in (pc cm^-3)^2; y x DM in pc cm^-3.

**DM PDF** ($z_s$ = 1, observer frame). Compound Poisson: every lightcone cell and impact node $b$ adds intensity $\lambda_b$ at $\mathrm{DM}(b)$ (cloud-in-cell on a 0.125 pc cm^-3 grid of $2^{16}$ points); $p=\mathrm{IFFT}[\exp(\mathrm{FFT}\,\Lambda-\Lambda_{\rm tot})]$, with $\Lambda$ the deposited intensity histogram and $\Lambda_{\rm tot}$ its sum. Quantiles use the mid-cell convention: the first cell is DM < 0.0625 pc cm^-3, so a quantile of 0.0625 means 'inside the no-crossing atom'.

**Halo crossings per sightline** [results/truth_summary.json: crossings].
$$\lambda_X=\sum_{z\le1}\sum_M N\,\frac{1-\cos(XR_{200c}/d_A)}{2},\qquad P(\text{no crossing})=e^{-\lambda_X}$$
- $X R_{200c}/d_A$: angular radius of the sphere [rad]. $\lambda$ = 1.058 / 4.234 / 16.93 for X = 1 / 2 / 4.

**Importance.**
$$I_O[b,s]=\frac{1}{k_O\,O}\frac{\partial O}{\partial\ln F_{b,s}},\qquad \sum_{b,s}I_O[b,s]=1$$
- $O$: an observable; $k_O$ = 2 for quadratic observables (variance, $C_\ell$), 1 for means, quantiles and each side of y x DM; $\partial/\partial\ln F_{b,s}$ scales every halo of bin $b$ in shell $s$ by $1+\epsilon$; the sum rule is Euler's theorem for homogeneous functions.
- Quantiles (Mecke): $\frac{\partial Q_p}{\partial\ln F_{b,s}}=\frac{1}{p(Q_p)}\sum\lambda_{b'}\,\frac{\partial\mathrm{DM}_{b'}}{\partial\ln F_{b,s}}\,p(Q_p-\mathrm{DM}_{b'})$, summed over cells and impact nodes $b'$; $Q_p$ the quantile; $p$ the full PDF of the summed DM, including the atom $\pi_0\,\delta(\mathrm{DM})$ of sightlines that cross no halo ($\pi_0=e^{-\lambda}$). The atom contributes $\pi_0$ times the $\lambda$-weighted density of single-halo DM values at $Q_p$ (a halo whose own DM equals $Q_p$ on a sightline that crosses nothing else); with it the identity $\sum\lambda\,\mathrm{DM}\,p(Q-\mathrm{DM})=Q\,p(Q)$ holds and the map of every quantile outside the atom sums to 1 (every sightline DM is linear in the profile, so $Q_p(\alpha F)=\alpha Q_p(F)$).
- On the CIC grid (step $\Delta$ = 0.125 pc cm^-3) the atom is the node-0 mass $p_0\Delta$, a triangle of half-width $\Delta$ around DM = 0. The engine's and Test F's Mecke code evaluate $p(u)$ as `np.interp(u, grid, density, left=0)` (`kernels/engine.py:_quantile_jacobian`, `F_observables/f_pdf.py`), which keeps only its half at $u\ge0$. J1 now uses the whole-atom density $p_w(u)=p(u)+p_0\max(0,1-|u|/\Delta)\,[u<0]$, computed with f_pdf's own code and a reference whose density is shifted by one node (a zero at DM = 0) and whose quantiles are shifted by $+\Delta$ (`j_mecke_atom.py`; f_pdf and the engine are unchanged). A quantile inside the atom (D1's Q16) does not move: its map is not defined.

**Truncation curves.**
$$T_O(R_t)=\frac{O(R_t)}{O(R_D)},\qquad C_O(R_t)=\frac{O(R_t)-O(0)}{O(R_D)-O(0)}$$
- $O(R_t)$: every calibrated halo cut in 3D at $R_t$, fallback unchanged; $R_D$: the definition's boundary; $O(0)$: no calibrated halo; $R_X$: first radius where $C_O=X$; loss at $R_t$ = $1-T_O(R_t)$.

**Tolerance per 0.1 dex.**
$$\mathrm{tol}(x)=\frac{0.01}{|k_O\,i(x)\,0.1\ln10|},\qquad i(x)=\frac{\sum_{b\,\rm cal}I_O[b,s]}{\Delta\ln x_s}$$
- the coherent fractional error of all calibrated stacks over a 0.1-dex range around $x$ that changes $O$ by 1%; $i(x)$: importance per unit $\ln x$ in the shell containing $x$, $\Delta\ln x_s$ its width.

### J.6 Treatment of the unresolved cores (T2, T3, S82)

Relative change of every DM statistic (mean, variance, Q16-Q99.9, DM-field C_ell, y x DM) against T1, C14 [post/j_post.json: treatment_sensitivity_rel_change, derived_for_report.treatment_largest_change]:

- largest absolute change per variant: T2 0.87%, T3 (raw fine shells) 0.042%, S82 (only haloes above the painting threshold in 12.7-12.9) 0.087%.

**What this does and does not test.** T1, T2 and T3 all keep each halo's measured electron count inside $x_h$; they differ only in how that count is distributed inside $x_h$ (the core shape). The result is that the DM statistics do not depend on the core shape below $x_h$. It does not test whether the measured low-mass gas content, N_e(<x_h) and N_e(<R200c), or its split across R200c, is converged with mass resolution. That split is what drives D1: in 12.7-12.9 $x_h$ = 0.98 R200c and the median 2h at 2R is 1.32 R200c, and the low-mass bins carry 25.9% of D1's mean DM in C14 (D1 mean changed by -25.7% against the fallback). The mean DM is unchanged by construction under T2 (+1.9e-04 relative for D1). A resolution test needs a higher-resolution run (J.8).

### J.10 Recommendations

1. Paint every halo above the 6.6e12 Msun threshold with calibrated stacks (C14). The fallback is wrong in shape in 12.7-13.5: inside R200c the measured electrons are only 0.27-0.55 of B16, and these bins carry 43.4% of D0's mean DM.
2. For a halo-only DM comparable to Konietzka's TNG within-R200 catalogue and to HalfDome's SphericalChordDMProfile, use D1 (A2-ii gas in the 3D sphere r <= R200c, chord-limited line of sight): mean 53.3 pc cm^-3, median 17.5 pc cm^-3, and 34.7% of sightlines to z_s = 1 with no halo contribution.
3. Do not use D0 (A2-ii to 4R) or the 5R extension as a 'halo component': 44.5% of D0's mean DM comes from 2-4R, where the gas is mostly unbound field gas. Use D0 only for total halo-plus-local-field budgets; D2 (r <= 2 R200c, mean 0.555 x D0) is the intermediate choice.
4. For the physically bound halo gas use D3 (host-bound to 4R): mean 0.356 x D0, compact (R90 of the mean 1.61 R200c); truncating it at 4R is safe (bound gas beyond 4R <= 2.6% of that inside 4R, ESTIMATE).
5. The treatment of the unresolved cores does not matter for DM: T1/T2/T3 and S82 move every DM statistic by at most 0.87%. But these treatments keep each halo's measured gas inside x_h, so they test only the core shape, not whether the low-mass gas content and its split across R200c are converged with resolution. D1 depends on that split (x_h = 0.98 R200c in 12.7-12.9; low-mass bins carry 25.9% of D1's mean DM). Before relying on D1 below 10^13.5, compare N_e(<R200c) and N_e(<1.35 R200c) of the 10^13-10^13.5 stacks with the L1_m8 reduced z = 0 snapshot (about 81 GB, ESTIMATE), which covers the whole D1 extent there; 12.7-13.0 would need the full snapshot.
6. Keep the kSZ recipe (A2-ii to 4R). Bound-only gas would remove 56% of the 1-halo kSZ at ell = 1000 and 83% of the 2-halo term; the earlier first-order estimate overstated the 1-halo change (exact -58.3% vs first-order -79.4% at ell = 100, C10).
